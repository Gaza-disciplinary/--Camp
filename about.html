Who We Are

Building Discipline.

Gaza Disciplinary Camp is a youth development organisation based in Gauteng, Johannesburg, South Africa. We support young people aged 9 and above through guidance, education, skills development and leadership opportunities.

Our Purpose

Guidance. Growth. Opportunity.

We believe every young person deserves support, encouragement and opportunities to build a positive future.

Our Mission

Our mission is to guide and empower young people through discipline, character development, education support, practical skills training, mentorship and community participation.

We encourage self-control, responsibility, confidence, teamwork, respect and positive decision-making. Our aim is not to punish young people, but to help them recognise their potential and become people who bring positive change and values to society.

We are committed to providing a safe, respectful and supportive environment where young people can learn, grow and develop.

Our Vision

Lead with Purpose.

To become a leading youth development organisation in South Africa, developing disciplined, educated, skilled and responsible young leaders who positively influence their communities.
