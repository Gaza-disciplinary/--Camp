Why is the One Day Call-Out R1,500, the same fee as the 7-day programme? Although the call-out takes place over one day, the fee covers more than the time spent delivering the intervention. Gaza Disciplinary Camp must arrange and transport the disciplinary team to the client's location, which involves travel, preparation and operational costs. The service also includes one day of monitoring and follow-up after the disciplinary intervention to review progress, reinforce the guidance given and encourage positive behaviour. The R1,500 fee therefore covers team transportation, the on-site disciplinary service and the additional monitoring day.
