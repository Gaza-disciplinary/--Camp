Our Approach

We promote positive guidance, dignity, appropriate supervision and age-appropriate activities. Our aim is to help young people make better choices and see a positive path forward.

Child safety comes first. The camp’s approach is intended to guide and support young people, not to abuse, humiliate or victimise them. Participation should involve a parent or legal guardian, and activities should be safe, respectful and age-appropriate.
